# Healthy

No disease was indicated by the symptoms provided. This isn't a substitute for a professional
veterinary exam — an animal that looks healthy today can still be in the early stages of a
condition that symptom checklists alone haven't revealed yet.

This is a general educational summary for a learning project, not veterinary guidance —
see the project's disclaimer.

## Precautions

- Continue routine observation for any new or developing symptoms (fever, appetite changes, lameness, discharge).
- Maintain good hygiene, clean water access, and adequate nutrition as ongoing disease prevention.
- Keep vaccination and parasite-control schedules up to date per your vet's recommendations.

## Next steps

- Re-check symptoms periodically, especially if the animal's behavior or condition changes.
- Schedule routine veterinary checkups rather than waiting for visible illness.
- Contact a vet promptly if any new symptoms appear, rather than assuming they'll resolve on their own.
